FAQ

What age is BÖKS designed for?

BÖKS is designed for children aged 4 to 7. The game uses only visual symbols — no reading or typing required — so even a 4-year-old can play independently.

Do children need to know how to read?

No. BÖKS is built entirely around visual block commands and animated feedback. There is no text to read during gameplay.

What devices does BÖKS run on?

BÖKS runs in the browser on tablets, iPads, and modern smartphones. No app download is needed — just open the link and play.

Is BÖKS free?

During the playtest phase, BÖKS is completely free for selected families and schools. Early access members will also receive a special offer when the full version launches.

How does the school programme work?

We're looking for primary schools to test BÖKS with classes of children aged 4-7. Schools get free access, a short teacher guide, and a feedback session with the creator. Fill in the form above to express interest.

Is it safe? Are there ads?

BÖKS has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no social features. It is a closed, self-contained experience designed to be safe for young children.
