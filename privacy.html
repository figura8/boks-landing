Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

BÖKS is an educational puzzle game designed to help children develop problem-solving and logical thinking skills through play.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the BÖKS game or visit the BÖKS website at boksgame.app.

BÖKS does not require users to create an account or log in. The game does not display advertising, does not sell personal information, and is not designed to collect personally identifiable information from children during gameplay.

1. Information We Collect

Information collected by the BÖKS game

The BÖKS game itself does not currently require an account, name, email address, profile, or other personal information in order to play.

BÖKS does not currently use developer-enabled analytics, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting services, or Google Play Games Services.

Some information may be processed automatically by the operating system, app store, device platform, or technical infrastructure used to distribute or run the application. This processing is controlled by those third-party providers and is subject to their own privacy policies.

Information submitted through the BÖKS website

The BÖKS website includes an optional playtest/contact form.

If you choose to use this form, you may provide:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • information you choose to include in your message, such as details about a child, classroom, school, or intended use of BÖKS.

Submission of this information is voluntary.

The form is processed using Formspree, a third-party form processing service.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information about a child through the form.

Technical website information

When you visit boksgame.app, technical information necessary to deliver the website may be processed automatically by our hosting and infrastructure providers.

This can include information such as:

  • IP address;
  • browser and device type;
  • requested pages or files;
  • date and time of requests;
  • basic security and network information.

We do not use this information for targeted advertising or to build advertising profiles.

2. How We Use Information

Information voluntarily submitted through the BÖKS website may be used to:

  • respond to questions or messages;
  • manage playtesting with parents, teachers, schools, or other participants;
  • understand feedback about BÖKS;
  • improve the game and website;
  • provide support where requested.

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use personal information submitted through the website for targeted advertising.

3. Children's Privacy

BÖKS is designed to be suitable for children.

The game itself is designed so that children can play without creating an account or providing their name, email address, or other personally identifiable information.

Any website contact or playtest form is intended to be completed by a parent, guardian, teacher, school representative, or other appropriate adult.

We ask users not to submit unnecessary personal or sensitive information about children through the website.

If we become aware that personal information from a child has been submitted to us inappropriately, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

4. Third-Party Services

BÖKS may rely on third-party services to operate the website or distribute the game. These include:

  • Google Play for Android app distribution;
  • Formspree for processing website contact and playtest forms;
  • Cloudflare for website hosting, delivery, security, and infrastructure;
  • Google Fonts for font delivery on the website;
  • operating system and device platform providers.

These third parties may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

BÖKS does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

5. Data Retention

Information submitted through the BÖKS website is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • respond to the request;
  • manage a playtest or related communication;
  • maintain necessary records;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

Information that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymised where reasonably practical.

Third-party providers may retain information according to their own retention policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through the BÖKS website.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should therefore avoid sending sensitive personal information through the website contact form.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • request access to information held about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us at hello@boksgame.app.

8. Cookies and Tracking

BÖKS does not intentionally use advertising or behavioural tracking cookies.

The website hosting provider or other technical services may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for security, functionality, or delivery of the website.

If the website later introduces analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

9. International Services

Some third-party service providers used by BÖKS may process information in countries outside your own.

Where applicable, those providers are responsible for applying appropriate safeguards to personal information processed through their services.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as BÖKS develops or if our data practices, services, or legal obligations change.

The latest version will always be available at https://boksgame.app/privacy.

The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently revised.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, personal information submitted through the website, or would like to request access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact:

Email: hello@boksgame.app

Website: https://boksgame.app/
